The details
🔒
Personal use
ALWAYS ON
Your symptoms, labs, and patterns power your own dashboard and your doctor brief. This data stays with you. We never sell it.
Optional · Help others living with MPN
📊
Aggregate research
Your de-identified data joins thousands of other MPN patients to help researchers find patterns. Names, addresses, and identifying details are removed. Individual records are never shared.
You give: anonymized trends You get: insights from people like you
🔬
Specific studies
When a researcher asks for participants in a specific study (e.g., MPN PROGRESSion Registry), we'll show it to you. Each study is its own decision — you say yes or no, one at a time.
You give: per-study consent You get: advance notice of relevant trials
✉️
Direct contact
MPN Research Foundation and approved researchers can reach you directly about trials you're a good fit for, surveys, and ways to get involved. You can decline anything they ask.
You give: permission to be contacted You get: direct invitations
🛡️
Always your choice. Change any of these in Settings. Withdraw at any time. We're transparent that no de-identification is perfect for rare diseases — which is why every step beyond personal use is opt-in.

How often do you want to check in?
Some people want deep daily tracking. Others want LED ready around their appointments. Pick what fits your life — you can change it anytime.
📊

"I want depth. Track everything."
· Daily check-in prompts
· Full symptom + wearable tracking
· Pattern insights as they emerge
· Best for: newly diagnosed, treatment changes, advocates
☀️
Steady companion
"A few times a week, light touch."
· Weekly check-ins, gentle reminders
· Symptoms when you want to log them
· Patterns surfaced when meaningful
· Best for: most patients, most of the time
📅
Appointment ready
"Mostly around my appointments."
· Minimal notifications between visits
· Brief auto-generated before appointments
· Quick check-in when you open the app
· Best for: stable patients, busy lives
